A Legacy of Hospitality

Hi, I am Richard Dauz, the current operations manager of Pacific Trails Resort and the grandson of the late founder, Antonio Lata Dauz, who served the community through hospitality.

At the age of 18, my grandfather moved to New York as a Filipino immigrant. He worked hard as both a busboy in a hotel and a limousine driver. Hospitality has always been an important part of Filipino culture, so in many ways, he fit right in.

Through his years of working in the hospitality field, he gained valuable experience and saved enough money to pursue the American Dream. After spending many years in New York, he eventually moved west to San Francisco.

From there, he later bought land along the coast of Northern California, where he built Pacific Trails Resort in 1985.

My grandfather had been serving the community through hospitality since the 1950s, and Pacific Trails Resort has proudly continued that tradition since 1985.

Today, we continue to serve our community and welcome new generations of guests as our family's legacy of hospitality carries on. We remain committed to creating a place where families can relax, enjoy nature, and always feel welcome.
