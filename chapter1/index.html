My favorite beverage is a caramel macchiato. It's sweet and milky. It consists of espresso with creamy milk, vanilla-flavored syrup, and a drizzle of caramel on top. A caramel macchiato is my favorite because I usually have a sweet tooth, and I also need the caffeine boost to start my day. I'd recommend this to all if they like desserts and coffee. I can get it at any café or coffee shop.
